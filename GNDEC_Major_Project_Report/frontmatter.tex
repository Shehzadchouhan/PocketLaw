\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\doublespacing

Access to timely, accurate, and affordable legal counsel remains one of the most formidable socioeconomic challenges across the Republic of India. With over 50 million cases pending across subordinate, High Court, and Supreme Court dockets, ordinary citizens facing everyday legal distress---ranging from cyber fraud, consumer exploitation, arbitrary tenancy eviction, and domestic disputes---are severely constrained by economic hurdles, systemic procedural obscurity, and multilingual communication barriers. Compounding this systemic crisis, on 1 July 2024, the Indian legal architecture underwent its most monumental structural overhaul since independence: the colonial-era Indian Penal Code 1860, Code of Criminal Procedure 1973, and Indian Evidence Act 1872 were completely replaced by the modernized \textbf{Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023}, \textbf{Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023}, and \textbf{Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) 2023}. Contemporary commercial large language model (LLM) platforms routinely fail Indian citizens by either hallucinating repealed colonial statutes, hallucinating fictitious precedents, leaking confidential legal disputes over public cloud APIs, or requiring expensive recurring subscription fees that remain out of reach for marginalized litigants.

To address these acute societal and technological challenges, this Major Project work presents \textbf{PocketLaw}, an end-to-end, privacy-preserving, and academically verified Legal Intelligence and Court-Ready Document Drafting Platform. PocketLaw unites offline Information Retrieval (IR) through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), statutory cross-mapping, hybrid artificial intelligence synthesis, and server-side vector PDF document compilation into an accessible, responsive web application.

The core retrieval engine is architected around a 15,000-dimensional sparse Vector Space Model (VSM) operating over a curated knowledge base of 2,290 verified text chunks extracted across 148 landmark Supreme Court of India judgments. In contrast to computationally expensive deep dense embedding models requiring continuous internet connectivity and proprietary vector databases, PocketLaw formulates a mathematically rigorous sublinear Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF) feature matrix represented via Compressed Sparse Row (CSR) matrices with a calibrated cosine similarity kernel ($\tau = 0.05$). This offline architectural paradigm achieves a 99.08\% matrix sparsity, ensuring ultra-low memory utilization while delivering an instantaneous average query latency of 41.6 milliseconds on standard commodity personal computer hardware. Empirical evaluation conducted via an automated 10-query academic benchmark demonstrates exceptional information retrieval accuracy, recording a Mean Precision@3 of 90.0\%, a Mean Precision@5 of 84.0\%, and a Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) of 0.92.

At the generative application layer, PocketLaw implements a resilient hybrid synthesis pipeline. When internet access and Google Gemini credentials are provisioned, the system generates grounded executive legal summaries constrained by strict prompt boundary guardrails that prohibit external legal fabrication. Conversely, in disconnected environments, PocketLaw seamlessly falls back to 100\% offline extractive summarization paired with a comprehensive, verified Indian legal ontology covering modern BNS/BNSS/BSA enactments, fundamental constitutional safeguards, emergency helpline directories, and step-by-step procedural playbooks across English, Hindi, Punjabi, and Urdu.

Complementing its consultative intelligence, PocketLaw incorporates a court-ready document generation engine capable of compiling formal First Information Reports (FIRs) under Section 173 BNSS (incorporating Zero FIR mandates), Section 80 CPC Formal Legal Notices, Notarized Sworn Affidavits, and Section 35 Consumer Complaints formatted for national E-Daakhil filing. The engine features dual-tier drafting: client-side reactive keystroke preview and server-side vector PDF compilation utilizing ReportLab flowables, official judicial headers, jurisdictional clauses, structured party tables, and formal two-column Notary Jurat attestations.

The persistence tier is governed by a fully normalized five-table relational SQLite 3 database managed through SQLAlchemy ORM, configured with Write-Ahead Logging (`PRAGMA journal_mode=WAL`), connection pooling, and cascade foreign key constraints, guaranteeing strict ACID compliance and robust read-write concurrency without external database server overhead. The front-end interface is engineered as a modern Single Page Application (SPA) leveraging React 18, React Router v6, Axios interceptors, Lucide vector icons, and Chart.js analytical visualizations.

The entire system has been validated through an automated regression and integration test suite comprising 16 unit tests across database transactions, PDF binary synthesis, statutory existence, and cosine ranking metrics, attaining a 100\% pass rate. PocketLaw demonstrates that mathematically sound, localized algorithmic design can democratize legal empowerment, eliminate statutory hallucination, and bridge the digital justice divide in modern India.
