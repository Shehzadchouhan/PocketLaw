- **Text Block:**
  > **The Judicial Access Divide:** Over 50 Million litigations remain pending across Indian courts. Ordinary citizens facing everyday crises (cyber skimming, unlawful eviction, matrimonial disputes) are crippled by prohibitive advocate retainers (₹3,000–₹25,000) and intimidating archaic terminology.  
  >  
  > **The BNS 2024 Transition Dilemma:** On 1 July 2024, the Republic of India repealed colonial-era penal codes (IPC 1860, CrPC 1973, Evidence Act 1872) and instituted the modern *Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)*, *BNSS*, and *BSA*. Citizens and police officers alike struggle with statutory renumbering and procedural cross-mappings.  
  >  
  > **Limitations of Existing Portals:** Traditional databases (Indian Kanoon) offer complex 50-page raw judgments, commercial portals enforce referral paywalls, and general LLMs (ChatGPT) frequently hallucinate repealed IPC sections.

- **Algorithmic Breakdown:**
  > **Algorithmic Formulation:**  
  > • **Sublinear TF-IDF:** $w(t, d) = (1 + \ln(\text{tf})) \cdot \ln(1 + N/\text{df})$ *(dampens repeated legal terms)*.  
  > • **Cosine Similarity:** $\text{sim}(q, d) = (\mathbf{q} \cdot \mathbf{d}) / (\|\mathbf{q}\| \|\mathbf{d}\|) \ge \tau\ (0.05)$ across 2,290 judicial chunks.  
  > • **Performance:** 41.6ms average query latency on CPU; 100% offline fallback without cloud APIs.

## 5. Printing & Mounting Guidelines (GNDEC CSE Notice)

1. **A3 Glossy Laminated Print:**
   - Use `PocketLaw_Project_Poster_A3.pdf` directly at any professional digital press.
   - Request **300 GSM Art Card Paper** with **Gloss Lamination** in standard **A3 (`297 mm × 420 mm`)** size.
2. **A4 Secondary Copy:**
   - The vector PDF scales with zero quality loss to **A4 (`210 mm × 297 mm`)** by selecting *Fit to Printable Area* in the print dialog.
3. **Canva Import:**
   - If adjusting in Canva, create a custom canvas of `3508 × 4961 px`, drag-and-drop `PocketLaw_Project_Poster_A3.png`, and place text or shapes over it.
